Urban Company Home Services

Urban Company is a technology platform that connects customers with trained service professionals for home and personal services.

Home Cleaning:
Urban Company provides several home cleaning services including bathroom cleaning, kitchen cleaning, full home cleaning, sofa cleaning, carpet cleaning, and specialized cleaning services. Customers can select a service and schedule a convenient time.

Beauty Services:
Customers can book beauty and wellness services at home. These may include salon services, hair care, skin care, manicure, pedicure, waxing, and makeup services.

Repair Services:
Customers can book repair and maintenance services for appliances and household equipment. Common categories include AC repair, washing machine repair, refrigerator repair, RO service, and electrical services.

AC Services:
AC services can include AC cleaning, AC repair, AC installation, AC uninstallation, and gas-related services. Customers should provide the appliance details and select a suitable appointment slot.

Service Booking:
A customer generally selects a service category, chooses the required service, provides the location, selects an available appointment slot, and confirms the booking.

Customer Support:
Customers may contact support when they have problems with a booking, payment, cancellation, rescheduling, or service quality.

Cancellation:
Customers should check the cancellation policy applicable to their selected service before cancelling a booking. Cancellation charges may depend on the service and timing.

Safety:
Customers should verify service details and appointment information before confirming a booking. Service professionals are expected to follow applicable safety and service guidelines.

RAG Question Answering:
